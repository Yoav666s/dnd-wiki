description: |
  You create a gravity field in a 20-foot Sphere centered on a point within range. Each creature that starts its turn in the sphere or enters it for the first time in a turn must succeed a Strength saving throw or fall prone. A prone creature within the sphere cannot use its movement to get up unless it also uses its action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC and succeeds.
  
  Alternatively, you can cast this spell over the course of 1 minute. If you do, the spell lasts for 8 hours, doesn't require your concentration, and the gravity inside the sphere is set to Low, Normal, or High, as detailed below.
  
  **Low.** Jump height and carrying capacity are doubled, fall damage is halved.
  
  **Normal.** Jump height, carrying capacity, and fall damage are normal.
  
  **High.** Jump height and carrying capacity are halved, fall damage is doubled.
  
  If you cast this spell over the course of 1 minute on the same spot every day for a year at the same slot level, the gravity field becomes permanent.
  
  **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the radius of the sphere increases by 10 feet for every slot level above 2nd.
