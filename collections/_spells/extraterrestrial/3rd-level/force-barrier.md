description: |
  You attach the emitter used as a material component for this spell to a willing creature who isn't wearing armor (possibly yourself), and a protective force barrier surrounds it until the spell ends. The target's base AC becomes 15 + its Dexterity modifier. The spell ends if the target dons armor, if the emitter is destroyed, or if you dismiss the spell as an action.
  
  **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the base AC increases by 1 for every two slot levels above 3rd.
