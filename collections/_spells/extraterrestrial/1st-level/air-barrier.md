description: |
  Choose a willing creature within range (possibly yourself). A protective magical force surrounds the target and traps the air around it for the duration. The atmosphere inside the space is composed of breathable air of a composition you decide, and the composition must be one you know. If you don't know any atmosphere composition, the air trapped inside remains the same. If the air composition trapped is one the target can breathe, the target can breathe normally regardless of its surrounding atmosphere or lack thereof. If the air trapped inside the barrier isn't compatible with the target's body, it begins or continues suffocating unless it is holding its breath. 
  
  This spell has no effect on creatures that don't need to breathe. The air trapped inside the barrier is being continually refreshed. The barrier only blocks nonmagical gasses. Liquids and all other physical objects, creatures, and effects can pass through it freely. The spell ends early if you or the target dismiss it (no action required).

  **At higher levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The air composition can be different for each one.
