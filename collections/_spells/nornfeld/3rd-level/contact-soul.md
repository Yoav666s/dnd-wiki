description: |
  You initiate a conversation with the soul trapped in the reliquary used as the material component for this spell. The spell fails if the soul was a target of this spell in the past 10 days.
  
  Until the spell ends, you can telepathically ask the soul up to five questions, to which it can reply telepathically. The soul knows only what it knew in life, including the languages it knew. The soul is under no compulsion to offer a truthful answer if you are antagonistic toward it or it recognizes you as an enemy. If the soul knew you in life, it recognizes you as the speaker. After you ask it five questions, you can ask more, but the soul is under no compulsion to give you any answer at all. Aside from asking the soul questions, you can communicate with it telepathically for the duration. The soul may or may not reply.
