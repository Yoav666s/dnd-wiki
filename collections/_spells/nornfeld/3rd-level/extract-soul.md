description: |
  Choose a humanoid body that has been dead for no longer than 1 minute. The target's soul becomes trapped in the reliquary used as the material component for this spell. The reliquary can hold a number of souls equal to your spellcasting modifier. While the soul is trapped in the reliquary, resurrection spells of 3rd level or lower such as Revivify don't work on the body. When the reliquary is opened or destroyed, any soul that was trapped inside can decide to pass on to afterlife or return to the Material Plane as a Ghost to haunt you. A Ghost that haunts you is hostile to you and your companions, and passes on to afterlife if you die.
  
  **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target an additional corpse for every slot level above 3rd. If you extract more souls than you can cointain, any excess souls pass on to afterlife.
