description: |
  You create a ghostly image of a Medium or smaller creature you have seen before in an unoccupied space you can see within range. It is obviously spectral, translucent, and cannot be mistaken for a living creature.
  
  When you cast this spell and as an action on subsequent turns, you can move the illusion to an unoccupied space you can see within range and cause it to make ghostly sounds such as unintelligible whispers or sorrowful wails, but you can't make it speak or act aggressively. As the illusion moves, you can animate it to make its movement seem natural. If you create a second ghostly image, the previous one fades away. If you move out of range of the ghostly image, it fades away and the spell ends.
  
  Things can pass through the image. Physical interaction does not automatically reveal the image to be an illusion, because things can also pass through ghosts. If a creature uses its action to examine the ghostly image, the creature can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the illusion becomes faint to the creature.
