description: |
  Whenever you expend 5 or more feet of movement in order to move, you can instead teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within a range equal to the expended movement.
  
  **At higher levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd or 4th level, you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 1 minute. When you use a spell slot of 5th or 6th level, you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 10 minutes. When you use a spell slot of 7th or 8th level, you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 1 hour. When you use a spell slot of 9th level, you can maintain this spell for up to 8 hours and it doesn't require your concentration.
