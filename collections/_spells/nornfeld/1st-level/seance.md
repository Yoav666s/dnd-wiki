description: |
  You attempt to communicate with wandering spirits, using a spirit board and a pointer. You and up to five other creatures place one or two fingers on the pointer. You and the other participants can ask up to ten questions out loud. Whenever someone asks a question, a wandering spirit will cause the pointer to move across the board, spelling out a short response. The spirit has knowledge of the immediate 300-foot area, knows what it knew in life, and is under no compulsion to tell the truth.
  
  When this spell is cast more than once in the same area within the span of an hour, spirits tend to get bored and mess with the participants. They might spell out swear words, confusing and cryptic messages, or ridiculous notions.
