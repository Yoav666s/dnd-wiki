description: |
  You create an illusory duplicate of yourself which appears in an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet and lasts for the duration. The duplicate vanishes if you're more than 30 feet away from it.
  
  When you cast this spell and as an action on subsequent turns, you can cause any number of duplicates created by this spell to move to another spot within range. As the image changes location, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural. Similarly, you can cause the illusion to make different sounds at different times, even making it carry on a conversation, for example.
  
  The duplicate’s AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier. If an attack hits the duplicate, the duplicate is destroyed. A duplicate can be destroyed only by an attack that hits it. It ignores all other damage and effects. If an attack misses the duplicate, it appears to dodge or evade it.
  
  Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, for things can pass through it. A creature that takes the Study action to examine the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the image, and its other sensory qualities become faint to the creature.
  
  **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you create one additional duplicate for each slot level above 1st.
