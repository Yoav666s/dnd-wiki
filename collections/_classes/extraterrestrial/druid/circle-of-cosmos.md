# Alien Shape
Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Wild Shape as a bonus action, and you can transform into an alien creature you've seen, even if it is an Aberration or a Monstrosity. Normal Max. CR and other limitations still apply.

# Circle Spells
Starting at 2nd level, your understanding of the cosmos imbues you with the ability to cast certain spells. At 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain access to circle spells connected to extraterrestrial travel.

Once you gain access to a circle spell, you always have it prepared, and it doesn't count against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you gain access to a spell that doesn't appear on the druid spell list, the spell is nonetheless a druid spell for you.

| Druid Level | Spells |
| :---: | :---- |
| 2nd | Air Barrier, Analyze |
| 3rd | Locate Animals or Plants, Protection from Poison |
| 5th | Sending, Tongues |
| 7th | Freedom of Movement, Transmit Image |
| 9th | Scrying, Teleportation Circle |

# Interspecies Communication
Starting at 2nd level, you can converse with alien species using gestures, even if you don't share a language.

Additionally, you learn the Minor Illusion Cantrip, and it counts as a druid spell for you. When you cast Minor Illusion and create an image, you can create a miniature image of anything you've seen, including creatures, that fits in you hand. It is obviously illusory and cannot be mistaken for a real, living creature, but it can be used to convey specific ideas.

# Alien Supremacy 
Starting at 6th level, you ignore the Max. CR column of the Beast Shapes table and instead you can transform into a beast or alien monstrosity/aberration with a challenge rating as high as your druid level divided by 4, rounded down. Other limitations still apply. Additionally, your attacks in alien form count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

# Wormhole Jumper
Starting at 10th level, while in alien form, you can use your bonus action to teleport up to a distance equal to half your form's movement speed to an unoccupied space you can see. 

# Gruesome Attacks
Starting at 14th level, once per turn while in alien form, an attack you make deals an additional 3d8 damage of the attack's type.
