Your connection to the Feywild grants you the following benefits.

- You can speak, read, and write Sylvan.
- You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
- You always have the [[Find Familiar]] spell prepared, and you can cast it an as action without expending a spell slot or material components. Once you cast it in this way, you can't do it again until you finish a long rest. When you cast Find Familiar in this way, the familiar must be a fey, and it can also take the form of a [[monsters:Spriteling]] or a [[monsters:Blink Pup]].
