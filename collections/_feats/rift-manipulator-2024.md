*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

**Cantrip.** You learn the Tiny Wormhole cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).

**Manipulator's Deftness.** When you make a Dexterity (Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, or Stealth) check, you can roll 1d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check.
