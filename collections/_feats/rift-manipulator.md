Your agility and dabbling in dimensional magic grant you the following benefits:

- You learn the Tiny Wormhole cantrip, and Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
- When you make a Dexterity (Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, or Stealth) check, you can roll 1d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check.
