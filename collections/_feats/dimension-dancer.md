You gain the following benefits.

- Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You always have the [[Dimension Slip]] spell prepared, and you can cast it without a spell slot or components at the spell level specified in the Dimension Dancer Spell Level table, based on your proficiency bonus. Once you cast it in this way, you can't do it again until you finish a Long Rest.
- When you fail a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration on [[Dimension Slip]], you can roll up to two of you unexpended Hit Point Dice and add the total rolled to your save, potentially turning the failure into a success. The rolled Hit Dice are then expended.
