This adventure is meant for one PC (Player Character) and a DM (Dungeon Master). This style of play is called a Duet. It's an adventure in the Silentspire Manor duet series, following the events of Silentspire Manor: First Steps, assuming the player didn't hire guards and was knocked unconscious by the thug and then kidnapped. This adventure uses 5e (2014) statblocks but is fully compatible with 5.5e (2024).
